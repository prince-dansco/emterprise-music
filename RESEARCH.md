# Phase 1-3: Enterprise AI-Powered Music & Video Distribution Platform

## Phase 1: Industry Research
**Competitors Analyzed:** DistroKid, TuneCore, CD Baby, ONErpm, UnitedMasters, Ditto Music, LANDR, Stem, Too Lost, RouteNote, SoundOn, Believe, Amuse, Symphonic.

### Key Findings:
- **DistroKid:** Great for speed and unlimited uploads, but lacks advanced marketing, analytics, and royalty splitting is basic. Customer support is historically poor.
- **TuneCore:** Good global reach, but expensive renewal fees per release.
- **UnitedMasters:** Strong brand partnerships and sync opportunities, but takes a high percentage (10%) on the free tier.
- **LANDR:** Good AI mastering integration, but distribution feels like an add-on rather than the core product.
- **Stem:** Excellent for royalty splits and financial clarity, but highly exclusive/invite-only.

## Phase 2: Discover Opportunities
**Creator Pain Points:**
1. **Metadata Rejections:** Uploads rejected due to formatting errors.
2. **Marketing Fragmentation:** Creators have to use 5 different tools to promote a single release.
3. **Financial Uncertainty:** Unpredictable royalty payouts and delayed reporting.
4. **Copyright Issues:** False copyright claims on platforms like YouTube.

**Opportunities Ranked:**
1. **AI Release Manager (High Impact, Med Complexity):** Pre-validate metadata, suggest release dates.
2. **AI Marketing Studio (High Impact, High Complexity):** Generate cross-platform campaigns automatically.
3. **AI Royalty Forecasting (High Impact, High Complexity):** Predict streaming revenue based on early data.

## Phase 3: Product Strategy
**Core Philosophy:** The platform should act as a virtual record label. It shouldn't just distribute; it should *manage, market, and monetize*.

**Key Differentiators:**
- **AI-First Workflow:** Every step from upload to promotion is assisted by AI.
- **Unified Ecosystem:** Combine distribution (DistroKid), marketing (HubSpot), design (Canva), and analytics (Spotify for Artists) into one seamless dashboard.
- **Proactive Intelligence:** Instead of showing a chart of past streams, the AI tells the creator *what to do next* (e.g., "Your track is trending in Brazil, click here to launch a targeted ad campaign").
